A portfolio of interactive dashboards at the intersection of healthcare data analytics and cybersecurity / HIPAA compliance, built on real public data from CMS, AHRQ, HHS/OCR, FDA, and CISA. Each project pairs an analytics question with a security or compliance lens — the combination that reduces both financial and regulatory risk for a healthcare organization.

Project 1 · Clinical Risk + Compliance

Hospital Readmission Risk & PHI Access-Audit Dashboard

Benchmarks 30-day readmission rates against real CMS/AHRQ national data to surface where avoidable readmissions (and CMS penalties) concentrate, paired with a PHI access-log audit panel that auto-flags after-hours and cross-department access.

  • Readmission analytics vs. real Medicare national benchmarks (HRRP)
  • Access-log audit flagging potential HIPAA improper-access events
